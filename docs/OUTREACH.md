# Outreach drafts

Status: draft only. Do not send, post or connect without Dev approval.

## Rules

- Send from Dev's own address, not a throwaway domain.
- One short email, one clear ask. No deck, no calendar link in the first message.
- Personalise the first line with something public: a case study, a service page or a recent post.
- Do not claim customer results. HandoffKit has no paying customers yet.
- Do not ask for a testimonial. Ask for a critique of one sample pack.
- Do not scrape private emails. Use the contact route on the agency site or the public profile.
- Track replies in the tracker below. Stop after one follow-up.

## Community post draft

Title: What do you actually hand over to an n8n client?

I build n8n workflows for clients and keep running into the same handover gap. The JSON is the source of truth for me, but it is not something most clients can read. The usual handover is a Loom, a few screenshots and a promise to call if something breaks.

I am testing a small tool that turns an n8n export into a plain-language handover pack. It covers the trigger, the steps, the systems, the credentials the client needs to own, the AI steps, failure paths and a maintenance checklist. It also produces a share link and PDF.

I am looking for three agencies to critique one sample pack, not to buy anything. What would you need in it to actually send it to a client? And what would make you reject it?

If this is already solved in your workflow, I would like to hear what you use.
