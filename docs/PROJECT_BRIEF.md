# HandoffKit project brief

Status: building an MVP, pre-revenue, pre-validation conversations.
Owner: Devayan Mandal (Dev), Mumbai.
Last updated: 2026-10-23.

## One line

HandoffKit turns an exported n8n workflow into a branded, client-ready handover pack that explains what the automation does, what data and AI it touches, and how to run it.

## Who it is for

Automation agencies and freelancers who build n8n workflows for clients. The buyer is the agency owner or delivery lead. The reader is the client's operations team.

The first import target is n8n. Make and Zapier imports are later, not part of this MVP.

## Pain

- A proper client handover takes hours of unpaid documentation work.
- The workflow JSON is not a handover document. Clients cannot read it.
- Credentials, failure modes and AI behaviour live in the builder's head.
- The client asks "what does this do, what data does it see, and what happens when it breaks" weeks later.
- AI steps add a new set of questions about models, personal data, human review and change control. Agencies are being asked for this by clients and procurement.
- The handover drifts as soon as the workflow changes.

## Outcome

An agency uploads an n8n export and gets a pack it can put its name on:

- Plain-language summary, trigger, steps and systems.
- Credentials inventory: type, name, nodes, owner guidance. Never secret values.
- AI register: node, provider, model, purpose, data categories, oversight, failure handling.
- Risk findings with severity and suggested fixes.
- Runbook: start, test, monitor, common failures, change process.
- Acceptance checklist and limitations.
- Branded share link, Markdown export and PDF.

## Offer

- Free: 1 pack, HandoffKit footer, share link, Markdown.
- Pro: $49/month or $490/year. Unlimited packs, custom branding, PDF and no footer.
- Target: 40 Pro customers at $49/month = $2,000 MRR. Annual and Agency tiers are later.
- Billing is not live until a Lemon Squeezy store, variant and webhook are configured and tested. Until then the pricing page collects early-access leads and says so.

## Why now

- n8n agencies are asking how to hand over workflows. Community threads on 16 and 21 October 2026 describe the problem from both sides.
- AI automation is moving from demos to client production, which increases documentation and procurement pressure.
- EU AI Act transparency expectations are phasing in, and clients are starting to ask agencies what AI a workflow uses and what data it processes. HandoffKit documents this. It does not claim legal compliance.
- Existing handover tools are early: a Chrome extension on a waitlist, a local free/Pro pack, and manual documentation services. None is a polished hosted pack with an AI register and client portal.

## MVP scope

In:

- n8n workflow JSON import by paste or file upload.
- Deterministic analysis. No LLM in the analysis path.
- Email/password accounts, sessions, tenant isolation.
- Projects, packs, public share links, Markdown, PDF.
- Branding: agency name, accent colour, optional logo URL.
- Free/Pro entitlements.
- Lemon Squeezy checkout and webhook integration behind environment variables.
- Landing page, live demo, pricing, terms, privacy, refunds.
- Activation events and a feedback form.
- Tests for analysis, entitlements, Markdown and PDF.
- Deploy to Vercel.

Out for now:

- Make/Zapier/Power Automate imports.
- Live n8n API connection and automatic re-sync.
- Team seats, roles, client comments, approvals.
- Legal compliance certification or advice.
- AI-generated prose.
- Native mobile app, marketplace, affiliate programme.

## Success metrics

Validation:

- 20 targeted conversations with n8n agencies.
- 5 real packs generated by unique agencies in the first 14 days after outreach.
- 2 paid commitments at $49/month, or 1 at $99/month, within 30 days of outreach.
- At least 40% of demo users who paste a real workflow create an account.

Product:

- Pack generation under 2 seconds for a 50-node workflow.
- No secret value appears in any pack, share page, PDF or event payload.
- Free limit enforced server-side, not only in the UI.
- Build, typecheck and tests green.

Revenue:

- $2,000 MRR. Count active paid subscriptions only. Do not count free users, one-time sales or verbal commitments.

## Kill or pivot rule

If 20 targeted conversations produce fewer than 3 real packs and zero payment conversations, stop building features and either sell a done-for-you handover service or kill the product. Do not add integrations to escape weak demand.

## Constraints

- Mumbai, India. Stripe India is invite-only and is not assumed. Lemon Squeezy supports India with PayPal payouts; bank payouts require a pre-approved Stripe account.
- No secrets in the repo. No customer data in logs.
- Every external claim needs a source and a date. No invented customers, testimonials or traction.
- Public copy: plain English, no em dashes, no AI voice.
- Deploy only after tests and a production build pass.
